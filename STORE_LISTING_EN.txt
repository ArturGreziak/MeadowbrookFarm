Meadowbrook Farm

SHORT DESCRIPTION
Grow a cozy farm, catch fish, and bring a quiet valley to life.

LONG DESCRIPTION
A patch of earth, a patient river, and a community waiting to know you. Welcome to Meadowbrook Farm, a cozy farming game where small daily choices help a little valley bloom.

GROW AT YOUR OWN PACE
Prepare your garden, plant carrots, tomatoes, and pumpkins, and give them a little water each day. Sleep when you are ready and return to watch your crops grow. There is no day timer, and your crops never wither. Rainy mornings even take care of watering for you.

MAKE THE FARM YOURS
Sell your harvest at the market, choose your next seeds, and gather wood and stone around the valley. Build a chicken coop for fresh eggs each morning or improve your well for more daily energy. Larger garden buttons make tending individual beds comfortable on smaller screens.

GET TO KNOW YOUR NEIGHBORS
Meet Mara, Eli, and June. Stop for a daily conversation or share a vegetable from your harvest to build friendship. When you feel like a change of pace, head to the dock and try the timing-based fishing challenge.

BRING THE VALLEY TOGETHER
Follow five journal chapters from your first harvest to restoring the town hall. Celebrate the Harvest Festival, then keep farming for as long as you like. Four changing seasons, animated water, and gentle rain give the valley its own rhythm.

Your progress is saved locally, so you can return to your farm later. Core gameplay works offline. On Android, optional rewarded advertisements can provide extra energy, while sleeping refills it for free.

Plant your first seeds and make Meadowbrook your home.
